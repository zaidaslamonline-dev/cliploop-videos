Maan lo abhi aap ek aise samay mein chale jao, jahan literally kuch bhi nahi hai... na dharti, na aasmaan, aur na hi time.

Aise hi shuruaat hui hamare universe ki. Ek kabhi na khatam hone wale, aur andhere se bhare anant sagar, yani Ksheera-saagara mein, sirf ek hi shakti maujood thi, jise Para-brahman kehte hain. Inhe na dekha ja sakta tha, aur na hi chhua ja sakta tha.

Magar... har andhere ke baad ujaala aata hai!

Anant sagar ki inhi lehron mein, hazaar phan wale Shesh-naag par, Bhagwan Vishnu Yoga Nidra mein leen the. Jaise hi srishti ki shuruaat ka waqt aaya, unki naabhi se ek divya, chamkila aur sunehra kamal nikla. Is kamal ne us ghane andhere ko cheer kar pehli baar ujaala laya... aur isi kamal par chaar mukhi Bhagwan Brah-maa prakat hue.
